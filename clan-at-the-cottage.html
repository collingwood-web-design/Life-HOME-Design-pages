Clan at the Cottage

Since the 1960’s this family has been cottaging at the lake for year-round fun.   With my client’s grandchildren now visiting (the fourth generation), it was decided the old structure had to go to make room for a larger and better functioning cottage.  The 2100 square foot bungalow, with its open concept areas is sized for the whole clan but feels intimate for two.  Spacious outdoor porches on the lakeside expand the living areas. The lower level has a TV area, bedrooms, and a bathroom – all with walk-up access to the lakeside – so handy for the busy ‘comings & goings’ of little ones.   The heritage of happy times continue!
