LAKESIDE MODERN

Memories are made here…. this large family enjoys summers at the lake and winters skiing at the local hills.  With several children to keep watch of, one item on the wish-list led the design direction – there had to be a view of the waterfront from all the principal rooms.  The spacious living/dining area can adjust as needed for extra friends and the dining area servery expands the functions for feeding a crowd.  Bedrooms are sized to be functional and outfitted with bunk beds.  Practical hard-wearing materials inside and out make for easy maintenance so that family fun can carry on!
