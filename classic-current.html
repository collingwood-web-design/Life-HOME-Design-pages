Classic & Current

How do we assess the success of a house design?  Does the home satisfy all the requirements on the original ‘wish-list’ from the client?  In a wider context, another gauge of successful design is how the home is perceived by others.  This classic ‘Manor-style’ home, with its understated elegance, traditional rooflines, and brick & stone gracefully sits beside a pathway leading to a community park.   The homeowner has kindly answered the door to over a dozen people inquiring about who designed & built the home.
