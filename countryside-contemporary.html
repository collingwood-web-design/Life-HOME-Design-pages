Countryside Contemporary

Inspired by the proportions of European farmhouses & barns, this home captures a solid and hard-working aesthetic.  The homeowner’s Netherlands heritage is imprinted inside and out.  Planning included practical spaces for main floor living and making entertaining easy – flowing to the outside Outdoor Living Area and pool.  The second floor has 2 bedrooms and a bath, the lower level houses over-flow for family and guests.  All levels are accessed by an elevator.  On the exterior, the hardness of the quarried stone from Wiarton and slate roofing is balanced with Douglas Fir arched beams at the porches.
