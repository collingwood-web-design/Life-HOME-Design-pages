Let It Snow

Located in snow country Stephen, Claire & family have been spending lots of time at their chalet.  The construction was completed, ready for the 2019 ski season.  Throughout 2020, with Covid-19 forcing restrictions on travel, school and work schedules, the chalet has multi-tasked as home, office, school, and vacation spot for this family of four!

Practical and hard-wearing, with a total of 3200 sq feet of living space on the main and lower levels, every inch of this chalet is used. A sheltered east-facing front porch leads to the bright entry with a spacious stair that joins the two levels. The open concept living, kitchen and dining area has 360-degree views to the surrounding rolling hills.

The master bedroom suite and a large mudroom (with direct access through the garage) & powder room occupy a wing linked to the main entry. With the lower level only 4 feet below grade, generously sized windows bring in lots of light and in-floor heating makes the area welcoming & comfortable.  At the lower level, there is a central recreation area, bathroom, laundry room and three additional bedrooms fitted out for sleeping the whole family.
